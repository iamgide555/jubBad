# Reusable Match Shuttles Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let opted-in groups track numbered, reusable physical shuttles across courts and games, with honest totals and bill sharing.

**Architecture:** A group switch is snapshotted on new sessions; ordinary sessions retain the current confirmation and end-of-night flow. Advanced sessions persist session-wide shuttle identities and per-game use links, while a single current assignment per active court prevents double booking. A pure aggregate and the existing bill engine derive distinct-used totals and cost shares; public read-only history and owner-only controls use the same data.

**Tech Stack:** Prisma/SQLite, NestJS/class-validator/Vitest, dependency-free TypeScript bill engine (`node:test`), Angular standalone components/signals and `ng test`.

**Spec:** `docs/archive/specs/2026-09-30-match-shuttles-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The group switch gates **D and E only**; it defaults to `false`, is snapshotted when a session is created, and never changes an existing session. F's configurable group ladder remains independent and defaults to today's levels; A and C remain available to all.
- Shuttle display numbers are monotonic, unique per session, and never recycled, even after a mistake is voided. Reusing a physical shuttle in multiple games counts it once in the distinct finished-game total.
- A new advanced game starts with one identity, selected by the host on manual confirm. Auto-confirm reuses the last available idle shuttle on that court or opens a new one; ordinary-session confirm is unchanged. One identity cannot be current on two active courts.
- A legacy/ordinary game has unknown use; an advanced game corrected to `[]` has **known zero use**. Undo finish retains its log; undo confirm clears the log but does not erase physically opened identities.
- `Session.shuttleCount` remains an independent nullable physical nightly count. For advanced shuttle-based billing, physical count takes precedence over a complete distinct finished-game count; do not infer zero from an empty finished-game set. For ordinary sessions, retain the physical-count-based split.
- With complete advanced logs, `byGames` splits **one effective total cost** among distinct identities, then their finished-game uses, then each game's players; `equal` is unchanged. Unknown logs with a physical total retain the old equal-per-match split. Never copy a bill with missing required inputs; zero is a valid input.
- D does not implement E's checkout. E must freeze early payments and reconcile later reuse against remaining players rather than changing a paid amount or forbidding reuse.
- Host writes are owner-guarded (owner mismatch 404), serialized per session, revision-checked and atomic across identity/use updates; public summary log is read-only. Host controls are at least 44px, Thai-primary with English translations, and the venue display remains unchanged.
- Feedback A must be implemented before executing this plan: its `labelForCourt` helper and numeric-keyed session labels are used by D. C may land before or after D; preserve its shared confirm blockers if present.

## Review Focus

1. Concurrent auto-confirms on two courts with one available shuttle must never assign it twice or issue the same new number (Task 3).
2. Undoing confirmation after opening shuttle 1 must leave it available or voidable, but the next new shuttle must be numbered 2 (Tasks 2 and 3).
3. Undoing finish after another court reuses its last shuttle must retain the old game's use without claiming that shuttle as current (Task 3).
4. A correction to an old finished game may add a shuttle now marked unusable, while a live court may not select it (Task 4).
5. An advanced session with zero finished games and no physical count cannot copy a shuttle bill; a finished known-empty game with zero price/count can (Tasks 5 and 7).
